Replace the legacy transfer-diff verification with the v5 authorization and effect verification pipeline. `simulate` now returns `Promise<VerifiedSimulationResult>`: the request is parsed, calldata is bound and decoded into `DecodedOperation`s, all inputs are pinned at the resolved block, effective limits are resolved, and a single `eth_simulateV1` executes probe reads, authorization preparations, and the user's transactions in one bundle — with one `AbortSignal` spanning every RPC.

Preview mode semantics: wallet requests (`erc20Approval`, `permit`, `permit2SignatureTransfer`) and Blue authority requests are *prepared* on-chain before the user transactions — approvals as owner `approve` calls, signatures as equivalent owner grants — and every prepared permission is read back and proven against the expected state. An unmatched, redundant, or insufficient authorization throws `AuthorizationRequestMismatchError`; a preparation that reverts or a permission that does not match the expected post-state throws `PermissionChangeMismatchError`. `simulationTxs`, `calls`, and `transfers` expose user transactions only: probe reads and preparation calls never appear in the public result.

Final mode performs a pure read-back: no authorizations may be supplied, and each signature form is checked against pinned state (allowance coverage, ERC-2612 nonce and deadline, Permit2 bitmap and deadline, `isAuthorized` or earlier-op satisfaction, Blue nonce and deadline).

Every decoded route now gets exact effect verification against the before/after snapshots accrued to the simulated block timestamp: Blue supply/withdraw/collateral/borrow/repay legs checked with blue-sdk share math and LTV/LLTV-buffer bounds, refinances checked leg-by-leg (source closed to zero, target collateral moved exactly, dust bounded by `limits.maxSlippageWad`), vault deposits/withdrawals/redeems/migrations checked against preview mint/burn amounts, VaultExitBundlesV1 force exits checked per deallocation leg including `forceDeallocatePenalties`, and in-kind redemptions checked per market leg. Referral fees reconcile to `FeeEvidence`; unexplained wallet, position, market, or adapter changes throw `AssetChangeMismatchError` / `StateChangeMismatchError`.

New typed errors that can now fire from `simulate`: `MissingVerificationEvidenceError`, `PermissionChangeMismatchError`, `MarketConstraintViolationError`, `SlippageLimitExceededError`, `FeeMismatchError`, `AssetChangeMismatchError`, `StateChangeMismatchError`, `ProtocolBindingMismatchError`, and `ConsumerLimitViolationError` (consumer `wallet.maxDebit`/`minCredit` and per-operation limits are now enforced). `UnsupportedVerificationFeatureError` remains exported but is no longer thrown by `simulate` — the preview-authorization and limits gates are removed.
