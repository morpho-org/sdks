`MorphoMidnight.supplyCollateralMakeBorrow` now runs as one atomic `MidnightBundlesV2.midnightBundlesV2CancelAndMake` transaction: it optionally cancels prior offer groups (guarded by `maxConsumed`), supplies one or more collateral transfers, authorizes and ratifies the tree root on the chain's PriceRatifierV1 or RateRatifierV1, and publishes the payload for `msg.sender`. Inputs change from `collateralAssets`/`collateralIndex`/`reservedCollateralAssets` to `collateralSupplies: { collateralIndex, assets }[]`, optional `cancellations`, and a required `deadline`; `offers` must be a `priceV1` or `rateV1` tree. Requirements are ERC-20 approvals and a Midnight authorization for `MidnightBundlesV2`; `buildTx()` takes no signatures and returns a `midnightSupplyCollateralMakeBorrow` action. Adds the `midnightSupplyCollateralMakeBorrow` builder and `EmptyMidnightCollateralSuppliesError`, `DuplicateMidnightCollateralSupplyError`, `UnsupportedMidnightBundlesV2RatifierError`. `MidnightBundlesV2` is now an accepted ERC-20 approval spender. No chain registers `midnightBundlesV2` yet; register a deployment with `registerCustomAddresses`. See `MIGRATION-v6-to-v7.md`.
