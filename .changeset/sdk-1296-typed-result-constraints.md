Consumer operation limits are now bound to exactly one decoded operation before anything executes. A limit that names an operation type absent from the bundle, a subject (`marketId`, `vault`, `authorized`, …) no operation of that type carries, several candidate operations without a `transactionIndex`, the same operation as another limit, or a bound weaker than the calldata protection (`maxLtvAfterWad` above the encoded `maxLtvWad`, `maxAssetsPaid` above `maxRepayAssets`, `minSharesMinted` below the encoded share-price floor, or any negative bound) throws `SimulationValidationError` with one `fieldErrors` entry per offender, keyed `limits.operations[<index>]`. The share-price floor is computed on the net deposit after the referral fee, and a zero encoded `maxSharePriceE27` is rejected the same way. Malformed limit values (a non-`bigint` bound, or a `minSupplyAssetsByMarket` entry that is not `{ marketId, minAssets }`) also throw `SimulationValidationError` instead of being skipped. Previously an unmatched or mismatched subject surfaced as `ConsumerLimitViolationError` and an unindexed limit silently bound the first matching operation.

Enforcement order is fixed: operation limits in operation order (transaction index, then call path); the first violated bound throws `ConsumerLimitViolationError`. A `debtFree` risk outcome (LTV 0, infinite health factor) now satisfies every cap and floor; only `unbounded` (zero collateral or liquidity) fails caps and passes floors. Previously `debtFree` failed every `max*` cap.
